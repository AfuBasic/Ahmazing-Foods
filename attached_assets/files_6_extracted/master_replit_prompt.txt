7. HEALTHY MEALS (renamed from "Mindful Meals" — if your build still
   has the old name anywhere, rename it: page file, nav link,
   footer link, homepage banner, all blog cross-links, and the
   Excel tab all need to say "Healthy Meals" now, URL is
   healthy-meals.html) — a build-your-own plan, not fixed packages.
   MUST include a visible disclaimer above the plan builder: not a
   medical treatment plan, doesn't replace a doctor/dietitian,
   consult a healthcare provider before starting.

   PRICING MODEL — per meal, not per full day. Every meal includes
   its own snack. Customer picks THREE things, in this order:
     (a) meals per day: 1, 2, or 3 (button group)
     (b) plan length, which sets BOTH the rate per meal AND the
         allowed range of days (button group):
           - Single Day — ₦20,000/meal — fixed at exactly 1 day
           - Weekly — ₦15,000/meal — 4 to 7 days
           - Two-Week — ₦14,000/meal — 7 to 14 days
           - Monthly — ₦13,500/meal — 14 to 28 days
     (c) number of days actually wanted, within that plan's allowed
         range (a number input, bounded live by whichever plan is
         selected — e.g. picking "Weekly" restricts this input to
         4–7; picking "Single Day" locks it at 1 and disables it)
   Total = rate × meals-per-day × days-chosen. The rate per meal
   does NOT change based on how many days within the range are
   picked — 4 days on a Weekly plan and 7 days on a Weekly plan both
   use ₦15,000/meal. The minimum-days floor on each plan (4/7/14)
   exists specifically so the discounted rate stays tied to genuine
   bulk commitment — without it, someone could take the Weekly
   discount for what's really just a couple of one-off days. State
   this reasoning on the page (there's an "advice box" for it), not
   just the numbers. Examples: 3 meals/day × 7 days on Weekly =
   ₦315,000. 1 meal/day × 4 days (the Weekly floor) = ₦60,000.

   RUSH FEE — applies ONLY to the first delivery drop, never the
   whole plan (a multi-week subscription being rushed as a whole
   doesn't make sense the way a single urgent meal does). Add a
   first-delivery date/time field using the EXACT SAME rules as Book
   a Meal: 9am-9pm hours, 12-hour minimum lead, 12-24 hour rush
   window, the same next-day-delivery auto-detection and notice, and
   the same cap logic (lesser of ₦20,000 flat or 50% of value) — but
   applied only to the value of the first drop specifically (rate ×
   meals-per-day × MIN(3, days chosen), since the first drop covers
   up to 3 days per the delivery logistics below). The rest of the
   plan is unaffected and bills at the normal rate.

   DELIVERY LOGISTICS — a full week of pre-cooked food in one fridge
   for 7 days is too long for freshness, so every plan delivers in
   TWO drops per week: Days 1–3 at the start, Days 4–7 mid-week.
   Meals arrive in fridge-safe, labelled containers (meal + day);
   customer refrigerates on arrival and reheats before eating.
   Two-Week and Monthly repeat this same two-drop pattern each week.

   The 15-item menu (3 breakfast + 3 lunch + 3 dinner + 6 snacks)
   remains individually orderable a la carte for anyone who doesn't
   want a plan — full data in AHmazing-Foods-FULL-Menu-Reference.xlsx,

   too, now with min/max days per plan). Snacks overlap with existing

9. BOOK A MEAL — now a CART, not a single-item form:
   - Customer configures a dish (category → dish → size → qty →
     protein) and clicks "Add to Order" to add it as a cart line.
     They can add more dishes before checking out. Each cart line
     is removable.
   - "Meals" for pricing purposes = number of DISTINCT DISHES in
     the cart, not total quantity/litres. Ordering 2L + 5L of the
     same soup is still one meal. Ordering Egusi (any amount) AND
     a different soup is two meals — they're two separate pots
     cooking at once, so they're priced separately.
   - Live order summary shows: cart lines with remove buttons,
     subtotal, rush fee line (only when applicable), and grand
     total — updates instantly as items are added/removed or the
     delivery time changes.
   - BUSINESS HOURS & LEAD TIME — deliveries only between 9:00 AM
     and 9:00 PM daily. A minimum of 12 hours' notice is required on
     every order — times sooner than that are not selectable at all.
     The time input's selectable range updates live: if the chosen
     date is today, the earliest selectable time is "12 hours from
     now" clamped to 9:00 AM.
   - NEXT-DAY DELIVERY CLARITY — if it's already too late in the day
     for the 12-hour minimum to fit before 9:00 PM closing (check
     BOTH whether the 12-hour mark rolls past midnight into tomorrow
     AND whether it lands later than 9:00 PM today — a time-of-day
     string comparison alone is not enough, since "8:00 AM tomorrow"
     and "8:00 AM today" look identical if you only compare the time
     portion), automatically move the date to tomorrow, set the time
     to 9:00 AM, and show a clear, friendly notice explaining why
     ("too late today for 12 hours' notice — moved to next-day
     delivery, earliest window 9:00 AM tomorrow"). Never leave the
     customer with a silently-impossible date/time combination.
   - RUSH FEE WINDOW — applies only between 12 and 24 hours' notice.
     Under 12 hours is blocked entirely; 24+ hours out has no rush
     fee. Base rate is charged PER DISTINCT MEAL, dropping as more
     meals are in the same order:
       1 meal=₦20,000, 2=₦15,000 each, 3=₦13,000 each, 4=₦12,000
       each, 5=₦10,000 each
   - RUSH FEE CAP — the rush fee actually charged is whichever is
     LOWER: the flat tiered amount above, or 50% of the order's own
     subtotal. This means a single cheap item (e.g. a ₦15,000 soup)
     is never charged a rush fee bigger than half its own price —
     it's capped at ₦7,500 instead of the full ₦20,000. Orders large
     enough that the cap wouldn't bind pay the same flat fee as
     before; nothing changes for those. Show which one applied in
     the UI (e.g. "rush fee capped at 50% of your subtotal") so it's
     never a mystery why the number is what it is.
   - "Meal" = distinct dish ordered — multiple sizes/quantities of
     the SAME dish still count as one meal
   - Order capped at 5 distinct meals; going over is blocked with a
     message pointing to Catering or Trays & Platters
   - GOOGLE CALENDAR (customer side) — on successful checkout, a
     "Add to Google Calendar" button appears, pre-filled with the
     delivery date/time, order summary, and address, using Google's
     calendar.google.com/calendar/render URL template. This works
     client-side, no backend needed — but it's one click, not fully
     automatic. To make it genuinely automatic when an email was
     given (zero clicks from the customer), the backend must, on
     payment success: (1) create the event on AHmazing Foods' own
     Google Calendar via a service account, (2) add the customer's
     email as a GUEST/ATTENDEE on that event through the Calendar
     API. Google then emails that guest an invite automatically —
     no OAuth or account access needed from the customer, because
     Google handles the invite step itself. This also solves the
     kitchen-calendar sync in the same step. This is backend-only,
     which is why only the one-click version exists in this static
     build.
   - ITEMIZED CONFIRMATION — checkout builds one message containing
     EVERY cart line (not just the first), each with size, quantity,
     and protein add-on, plus pepper level, delivery date/time,
     subtotal, rush fee breakdown, grand total, and the bank
     transfer details, and the customer's contact details — sent via
     WhatsApp so both the business and (by forwarding) the customer
     have a complete copy.
   - Required pepper-level slider (Low / Medium / Really Peppery),
     with the 3 positions visibly marked on the track (circles or
     boxes, not just a plain gradient bar) so it reads as a discrete
     3-position control. Checkout is blocked with a visible warning
     until the customer has actually moved it — a default value
     alone doesn't count as a real selection.
   - Paystack integration point clearly marked as a placeholder
     needing a real backend; Google Calendar's business-side sync
     likewise.

- BUG REPORTED BUT NOT REPRODUCIBLE IN THIS BUILD — clicking "Book
  Your Meal"/"Book a Meal" landing on the footer instead of the top
  of the booking form: tested directly (scripted click from the
  homepage CTA straight through to the destination page) and it
  loads at the very top every time in this codebase. If this
  happens on your build, check for: a stray `#` anchor somewhere in
  that specific link, an element with `autofocus` near the bottom
  of book.html, or a leftover `scrollIntoView()` call running on
  page load rather than only on the specific validation failures
  it's meant for.

- BUG REPORTED BUT NOT REPRODUCIBLE IN THIS BUILD — auto-scroll to
  footer on selection: tested directly (every category/item/size/
  protein change, add-to-cart, pepper slider move) with no unwanted
  scroll in this codebase. If this happens on your build, common
  causes to check: an errant `href="#"` link without
  preventDefault(), an element with `autofocus`, a `scrollIntoView()`
  call firing outside the one intentional case (failed pepper
  validation on submit), or native mobile browser behavior scrolling
  a focused `<select>` into view — test on the actual device/browser
  where it was seen and isolate which specific interaction triggers it.

SEO REQUIREMENTS (apply to every page)
----------------------------------------
- Exactly one <h1> per page, containing the page's primary keyword
  naturally (e.g. "Nigerian Soup Delivery in Lagos").
- Unique <title> tag per page (under ~60 characters where possible,
  include "Lagos" and the relevant dish/service).
- Unique <meta name="description"> per page (under ~160 characters).
- <link rel="canonical"> pointing to the page's own URL.
- Open Graph tags (og:title, og:description, og:type) on every page.
- JSON-LD Restaurant/LocalBusiness schema on the homepage (name,
  cuisine, phone, email, address locality "Lagos", region "Lagos
  State", country "NG").
- alt text on every image describing what it actually shows.
- A sitemap.xml listing every live (non-hidden) page, and a
  robots.txt pointing to it.

  and competition, and typically takes months to show results even
  when done well. Don't oversell this to the client as an instant
  fix.
- Every price in Naira (₦), formatted with commas.
- Every product card's "Order" button opens WhatsApp
  (wa.me/2348105506052) with the item name pre-filled in the
  message text.
- No image should ever be a stock photo or a competitor's photo —
  use real AHmazing photography where supplied, clearly-marked
  placeholders everywhere else.
- Mobile-responsive throughout; nav collapses to a hamburger with
  an opaque dropdown below 860px width.
