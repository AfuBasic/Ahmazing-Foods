Healthy Meals are prepared with lower sugar, lower sodium, and higher-fibre ingredients and cooking methods (grilling and boiling instead of deep-frying, less palm oil, whole grains, controlled portions) — choices commonly recommended for people managing diabetes or high blood pressure. This is a homemade meal service, not a medical treatment plan, prescription, or diagnosis, and it does not replace advice from your doctor or a registered dietitian. Please consult your healthcare provider before starting any new eating plan, especially if you take medication for diabetes or blood pressure.

Why the rate drops with commitment — and why there's a minimum

A single day is priced as a one-off, no-commitment order. The Weekly, Two-Week, and Monthly rates are lower per meal because we're planning and cooking in bulk — the longer the commitment, the more that saves us, and we pass it back to you. Each plan has a minimum number of days (Weekly: 4, Two-Week: 7, Monthly: 14) so the discount stays tied to genuine bulk planning, not just a lower price on a tiny order.

If your first delivery falls within 24 hours (minimum 12 hours' notice), a rush fee applies to that first drop only — the rest of your plan continues on the normal schedule at no extra charge.

How it's delivered

Cooked fresh, delivered in two drops a week

A full week of pre-cooked food sitting in one fridge for 7 days is longer than we're comfortable with for freshness. Instead:

1
First drop — Days 1 to 3

Delivered at the start of your plan, portioned and labelled by meal and day, ready to refrigerate.

2
Second drop — Days 4 to 7 (or next week)

Delivered mid-week so nothing sits in your fridge longer than a few days before you eat it.

Storage & reheating: meals arrive in fridge-safe containers, clearly labelled with the meal and day. Refrigerate on arrival, reheat before eating. Two-Week and Monthly plans repeat this same two-drop pattern each week of the subscription.
