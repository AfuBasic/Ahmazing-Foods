AHMAZING FOODS — FULL SITE BUILD SPEC
======================================
Build a simple, static multi-page website (plain HTML/CSS/JS, no
framework needed) for AHmazing Foods, a home-cooked meal and catering
business in Lagos, Nigeria. Everything below reflects the actual,
already-approved site — this is a rebuild/reference spec, not a
first draft.

7. MINDFUL MEALS — no longer just a browse-and-order menu. It's a
   SUBSCRIPTION meal-plan service: a full day (breakfast + lunch +
   dinner + 1 snack, rotated from the 15-item menu) delivered on a
   schedule. MUST include a visible disclaimer above the plans: not
   a medical treatment plan, doesn't replace a doctor/dietitian,
   consult a healthcare provider before starting.

   SUBSCRIPTION PLANS (price is per day, discount grows with length):
     - Single Day (no subscription) — ₦21,000/day
     - Weekly (7 days) — ₦19,000/day — ₦133,000 total — ~9% saved
     - Two-Week (14 days) — ₦17,500/day — ₦245,000 total — ~17%
       saved — marked "Most Popular"
     - Monthly (28 days) — ₦16,000/day — ₦448,000 total — ~24% saved
   No separate "daily" tier exists on purpose — ordering one day
   with no commitment is just the Single Day price above; a
   subscription's value is the discount for planning ahead, so the
   shortest real subscription is Weekly. State this reasoning
   directly on the page (there's an "advice box" for it).

   DELIVERY LOGISTICS — a full week of pre-cooked food in one fridge
   for 7 days is too long for freshness, so every plan (Weekly,
   Two-Week, Monthly) delivers in TWO drops per week: Days 1–3 at
   the start, Days 4–7 mid-week. Meals arrive in fridge-safe,
   labelled containers (meal + day); customer refrigerates on
   arrival and reheats before eating. Two-Week and Monthly repeat
   this same two-drop pattern each week.

   The 15-item menu (3 breakfast + 3 lunch + 3 dinner + 6 snacks)
   remains individually orderable a la carte for anyone who doesn't
   want a subscription — full data in AHmazing-Foods-Product-
   List.xlsx, "Mindful Meals" tab (now includes the subscription
   pricing table too). Snacks overlap with existing Products
   (Roasted Peanuts, Zobo, Tiger Nut Milk, Peanut Butter),
   repositioned as unsweetened/unsalted. Keep the 6-day sample
   rotation table showing how the menu avoids repeating.

8. CATERING — event quote request form: name, phone, email, event

9. BOOK A MEAL — now a CART, not a single-item form:
   - Customer configures a dish (category → dish → size → qty →
     protein) and clicks "Add to Order" to add it as a cart line.
     They can add more dishes before checking out. Each cart line
     is removable.
   - "Meals" for pricing purposes = number of DISTINCT DISHES in
     the cart, not total quantity/litres. Ordering 2L + 5L of the
     same soup is still one meal. Ordering Egusi (any amount) AND
     a different soup is two meals — they're two separate pots
     cooking at once, so they're priced separately.
   - Order cap: maximum 5 distinct meals per booking. Trying to add
     a 6th distinct dish is blocked with a message pointing to
     Catering or Trays & Platters for bigger orders. Adding more of
     an already-added dish is never blocked by this cap.
   - RUSH FEE: if the requested delivery date/time is within 24
     hours of booking, a rush fee applies — charged PER DISTINCT
     MEAL, at a rate that drops as more distinct meals are in the
     same order:
       1 meal  → ₦20,000/meal
       2 meals → ₦15,000/meal
       3 meals → ₦13,000/meal
       4 meals → ₦12,000/meal
       5 meals → ₦10,000/meal
     Total rush fee = per-meal rate × number of distinct meals.
     Outside the 24-hour window, no rush fee applies at all.
   - Live order summary shows: cart lines with remove buttons,
     subtotal, rush fee line (rate × meal count, only when
     applicable), and grand total — updates instantly as items are
     added/removed or the delivery time changes.
   - PEPPER LEVEL — a required interactive slider (range input,
     3 positions: Low / Medium / Really Peppery), styled with chili
     icons that fill in and grow as the level increases. Applies to
     the whole order, not per item. Track whether the customer has
     actually touched it (a default value alone doesn't count as a
     real selection) — checkout is blocked with a visible warning
     message and the slider highlighted until they've deliberately
     moved it at least once.
   - Paystack and Google Calendar integration are placeholders (need
     a backend — not wired live).

SEO REQUIREMENTS (apply to every page)
----------------------------------------
- Exactly one <h1> per page, containing the page's primary keyword
  naturally (e.g. "Nigerian Soup Delivery in Lagos").
- Unique <title> tag per page (under ~60 characters where possible,
  include "Lagos" and the relevant dish/service).
- Unique <meta name="description"> per page (under ~160 characters).
- <link rel="canonical"> pointing to the page's own URL.
- Open Graph tags (og:title, og:description, og:type) on every page.
- JSON-LD Restaurant/LocalBusiness schema on the homepage (name,
  cuisine, phone, email, address locality "Lagos", region "Lagos
  State", country "NG").
- alt text on every image describing what it actually shows.
- A sitemap.xml listing every live (non-hidden) page, and a
  robots.txt pointing to it.
- IMPORTANT — set honest expectations: none of the above guarantees
  a #1 Google ranking. On-page SEO is necessary but not sufficient;
  ranking also depends on backlinks, domain age, content freshness,
  and competition, and typically takes months to show results even
  when done well. Don't oversell this to the client as an instant
  fix.
- Every price in Naira (₦), formatted with commas.
- Every product card's "Order" button opens WhatsApp
  (wa.me/2348105506052) with the item name pre-filled in the
  message text.
- No image should ever be a stock photo or a competitor's photo —
  use real AHmazing photography where supplied, clearly-marked
  placeholders everywhere else.
- Mobile-responsive throughout; nav collapses to a hamburger with
  an opaque dropdown below 860px width.
