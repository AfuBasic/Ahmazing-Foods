Please read before ordering

Mindful Meals are prepared with lower sugar, lower sodium, and higher-fibre ingredients and cooking methods (grilling and boiling instead of deep-frying, less palm oil, whole grains, controlled portions) — choices commonly recommended for people managing diabetes or high blood pressure. This is a homemade meal service, not a medical treatment plan, prescription, or diagnosis, and it does not replace advice from your doctor or a registered dietitian. Please consult your healthcare provider before starting any new eating plan, especially if you take medication for diabetes or blood pressure.

Why there's no "daily" subscription

A subscription's value is the discount you get for planning ahead. Ordering one day at a time already works with no sign-up — that's the ₦21,000 single-day option above. We start real subscriptions at a week, because that's the shortest commitment where pre-planning your cooking and delivery actually saves you anything.

How it's delivered

Cooked fresh, delivered in two drops a week

A full week of pre-cooked food sitting in one fridge for 7 days is longer than we're comfortable with for freshness. Instead:

1
First drop — Days 1 to 3

Delivered at the start of your plan, portioned and labelled by meal and day, ready to refrigerate.

2
Second drop — Days 4 to 7 (or next week)

Delivered mid-week so nothing sits in your fridge longer than a few days before you eat it.

Storage & reheating: meals arrive in fridge-safe containers, clearly labelled with the meal and day. Refrigerate on arrival, reheat before eating. Two-Week and Monthly plans repeat this same two-drop pattern each week of the subscription.
